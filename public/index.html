    How to use Planet Pulse

    What you're seeing

    • Earthquakes. Bigger, redder circles are stronger quakes. A pulsing ring means it happened in the last hour. Magnitude is logarithmic, so each whole number is about 32× more energy.
    • Natural events. Wildfires, storms, volcanoes and icebergs that NASA is tracking. Each type has its own shape. Use the legend to show or hide types.
    • Day/night shading. The darker half of the globe is where it's night right now.
    • Kp index. Geomagnetic activity from 0 (calm) to 9 (extreme storm). At Kp 5 or higher, auroras may be visible far from the poles.

    Getting around

    • Drag to spin the globe. Pinch, hold Ctrl + scroll, or use the + / − buttons to zoom. A plain scroll moves the page, so you never get stuck on the map.
    • Click a marker for details, or click a country to see its population, economy and currency.
    • Use the search box to jump to any country, even tiny ones.
    • Click a headline number at the top to jump straight to it.
    • Your settings are remembered. Selecting a country updates the page address, so you can share the link.
